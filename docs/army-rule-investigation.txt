Army-rule coverage investigation - 2026-10-16

Scope and evidence
Audited all 36 factions in the live local library (read-only SQLite transaction)
and the shipped bootstrap. Inspected their cached BSData source archives and
traced shared import, matching, recovery and both frontend display paths.
No application code, library, army or bundled resource was changed. No live
download or current upstream rules verification was performed.

Bootstrap source: cc1830fbfead059f8059ed6c7f8e73cd4c390908, dated 2026-10-02.
All-faction inventory: docs/army-rule-coverage-audit.csv. Counts describe the

did not perform a new full import or launch each faction's native UI.

Confirmed empty army-rule sections in the live library
- Tyranids: Synapse and Shadow in the Warp are missing as army-rule cards.
  The source defines both in Library - Tyranids.json. The primary catalogue
  also links Synapse directly through its root infoLinks. Unit abilities retain
  Synapse on 59 imported units and Shadow in the Warp on 21.
- Astra Militarum: Voice Of Command is defined in its linked library and
  retained in 18 imported unit profiles, but no army-rule card is emitted.
- Drukhari: Power from Pain and Corsairs and Travelling Players are defined
  in the shared Aeldari library with explicit Drukhari applicability. Neither
  becomes a Drukhari army-rule card. Power from Pain remains on 25 unit profiles.
- Titan Legions and Chaos Titan Legions: Towering Example is imported, but
  marked Unresolved and hidden in each faction. Both have a valid catalogue
  identity in FactionContexts and zero detachment definitions.

Cause 1: rules discovery incorrectly follows native roster inheritance
BsDataImporter.Include loads all linked catalogues into the registry, so the
definitions are available. However, NativeCatalogues follows only links whose
importRootEntries is true and whose faction matches (or is a marine parent).
ReadReferences emits army-rule cards only from rules/sharedRules directly on
those native roots. It does not emit their root infoLinks as army-rule cards.
Tyranids and Astra Militarum link their own libraries without importRootEntries;
Drukhari links a differently named shared Aeldari library the same way. Their
rules therefore disappear from the army-rule reference collection while unit
profile extraction can still resolve the same definitions.

Cause 2: faction matching incorrectly requires detachment metadata
ReferenceMatching.Lookup builds FactionIds solely from data.Detachments and
ignores data.FactionContexts. A card with an explicit FactionId cannot fall
back to its name. Both Titan factions therefore fail exact identity matching,
despite having correct catalogue IDs. ReferenceMerge stores them Unresolved;
ReferenceIndex excludes them from both platforms' army-rule display.

Other findings
- All remaining 31 factions have at least one usable army-rule card. This is
  not a claim that every rule's completeness or applicability is correct.
- Space Marines and eleven chapters have Combat Doctrines because
  SpaceMarineArmyRule.Apply supplies a targeted recovery supplement. Raw
  bootstrap cards do not include Combat Doctrines. Their visible coverage
  masks the underlying library-rule discovery limitation.
- The inverse scope defect is visible in Aeldari: Power from Pain appears as
  an Aeldari army-rule card even though its source hides it outside Drukhari.
  ReadReferences assigns all native-root shared rules to the importing faction;
  conditions attached to rule effects do not filter reference card visibility.
- A cross-check of shared source rules against imported unit abilities also
  finds allied rules, weapon rules and model-specific abilities (for example
  Librarius and Transhuman Strategist). These cannot all be promoted safely
  into army rules merely because they occur on imported units.
- ReferenceCoverageAudit currently measures Stratagem scopes. The new bundled
  eligibility candidate gate measures Enhancements. Neither validates army-rule
  completeness or proves each faction's imported army-rule cards are displayable.
- This omission exists in both the older bootstrap and the newer live source
  import. Keyword search and battle-secondary collapse touch neither path.

Recommended generic correction (not implemented in this investigation)
Separate army-rule discovery from roster-root inclusion: resolve catalogue
references and linked definitions using explicit faction/catalogue applicability,
respect source visibility and detachment scope, and retain source identities.
Use FactionContexts for faction identity matching independently of detachments,
preserving ambiguity rejection and existing tombstone/override ownership.
Add source-to-import-to-display army-rule coverage validation, including
nonempty-but-incomplete and wrongly attributed collections. Regenerate bundled
data and provide provenance-aware recovery for existing imports. Avoid adding
another faction-specific supplement or copying every unit ability into army rules.
