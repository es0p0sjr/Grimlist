Equipment pricing and incomplete loadouts (2026-10-21)

Problem
The previous importer discarded a free-equipment loadout when any weapon profile
was unmapped. EquipmentPrices then returned an empty list, while the editor made
manual:<weapon family> choices. Those choices had no price rows, so choosing a
weapon made both its label and the unit total unavailable. The repair scan did
not detect empty lists. Boyz's nested Nob weapon bundles exposed this path.

Shared behavior
- ReadDatasheet retains mapped choices and source profile IDs. Unmapped weapons
  remain available in an explicitly labelled manual-review group; Incomplete
  and Loadouts.ReviewWarning prevent a partial mapping from implying legality.
- ManualEquipmentPrices reads the original source paths independently of the
  automatic editor. Every occurrence of a profile must agree. Complete source
  paths with no additional equipment cost yield explicit zero-price records.
  Simple paid selections retain their charge. Missing references, conflicting
  costs, equipment price modifiers and paid multi-weapon bundles stay unknown.
  Unit/copy/model price modifiers remain the responsibility of their existing
  calculators. Equipment modifiers affecting descendants remain unresolved.
- Manual option IDs and saved quantities are preserved. Existing manual entries
  can still be edited and rendered after an import gains automatic choices.
  No automatic redistribution of weapons across models is attempted.
- Pricing and legality are separate: known equipment can retain a numeric total
  while replacement limits require review. Unresolvable saved selections must
  not silently disappear from pricing. Legacy paid-equipment warnings remain
  protected. Existing implicit pricing for old non-BSData definitions is retained.
- PricingRepair detects selectable options missing from a non-null cost table.
  New source imports and repricing retain manual price records. Extra unresolved
  manual aliases do not invalidate a fully mapped automatic option's known price.

Existing libraries
EquipmentPriceRecovery applies a library projection through ArmySession, also
feeding the versioned faction cache. It adds missing manual price records only.
Existing prices/overrides, profiles/loadouts, army/entry IDs, saved equipment,
MFM base prices and source snapshots are preserved. Repeated application is a
no-op. There are no live database migrations or downloads in this recovery.
Units already carrying source-selection cost rows are left intact, since those
rows may contain overrides that newly introduced manual aliases could bypass.

Evidence requires matching edition, archive SHA-256, faction and BSData entry
alias, plus an exact weapon-content hash. Flattened legacy profiles lacking
source IDs/categories use a separately generated legacy hash of the same pinned
source's weapon contents. Edited profiles and conflicting evidence are skipped.
Unsupported source revisions retain unknown prices until suitable evidence is
provided; this is not a blanket zero-cost fallback.

Validation
The existing Test-BundledEnhancements.ps1 gate, used by both publishing scripts,
now also validates equipment coverage after recovery: every selectable bundled
option needs a numeric price or an explicit unresolved reason. It does not claim
that every source rule is supported, or that every loadout is legal.
EquipmentPriceChecks covers included/paid/conditional/ambiguous/missing/bundled
prices, empty-list detection, stale selections, source/override guards, legacy
profiles, persistence and actual pinned Boyz import plus bundled recovery.
Focused command: dotnet run --project tests/GrimList.Tests/GrimList.Tests.csproj -- --equipment-prices-only --lf-json
