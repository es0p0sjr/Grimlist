Imperium's Sword melee profile fix - 2026-10-16

Behavior
Assault Brethren's Imperium's Sword adds one automatically equipped melee
weapon to the selected, eligible bearer. Existing weapons remain equipped.
Windows and Android use their normal weapon profile display in build/play.
The enhancement explanation replaces the previously flattened raw stat lines.
Selection removal, detachment changes and invalid eligibility clear the grant.
The library's base unit and saved equipment selections are not rewritten.
The older The Imperium's Sword (1st Company Task Force) retains its original rule.

Implementation
ReferenceCard.Weapons retains the typed imported profile and source profile ID.
EnhancementWeapons reads existing BSData 11e Assault Brethren raw-stat cards
using their saved values, guarded by identity and expected format; no reimport
or live database modification is required. Explicit empty metadata is respected.
Other weapon enhancements are not automatically inferred as additive grants.
EffectiveDatasheets adds the weapon before normal weapon modifiers. GrantedBy
marks its automatic equipment count; Loadouts includes one copy in play/export
and excludes it from manually editable equipment choices. Enhancement selection
remains the persisted source of truth. Yellowscribe retains its guard against
exporting unit-level weapons with ambiguous ownership in multi-model entries.
