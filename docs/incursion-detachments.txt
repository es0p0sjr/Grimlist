1,000-point detachment allowance - 2026-10-24

User-requested behavior
At exactly 1,000 points, allow selected detachments totaling at most 2 DP,
or one detachment costing 3 DP. A 3-DP detachment must stand alone, even if
the second detachment is free. This is not a general increase to a 3-DP budget.
Unknown costs and unsupported game sizes remain unresolved. Faction/catalogue
eligibility, duplicate selection and incompatible combinations remain enforced.
Budgets at 2,000 and 3,000 points are unchanged.

Implementation
DetachmentRules.WithinBudget is shared by CanAdd and validation. Existing legacy
single Detachment fields are supported through the usual Selected projection.
Both platform setup/rules screens use shared budget summary text. Windows
selection previews and remaining-budget hints recognize the exception, and long
summary text wraps. Export explains the allowance instead of showing 3/2 DP.
No army persistence, library/source data or versioned packages were changed.
