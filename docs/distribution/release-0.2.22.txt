Limits
Windows MSI is unsigned. Android uses the existing development signing key.
Android physical in-place update/cold launch remains unverified for this release.
Windows install/repair/upgrade/uninstall lifecycle remains unverified; extraction
and welcome/cancel checks do not establish those operations. Broader device,
loadout legality and optical QR checks remain manual. Numeric points do not prove
completeness, and this release does not claim every rule or price is current.
