GrimList phone test - 2026-10-05

1. In Files, open Downloads > GrimList.
2. Tap GrimList-Android-Test.apk and install/update GrimList.
   Install over the existing app. Do not uninstall or clear storage, because
   that removes saved armies. Allow Files to install apps if Android asks.
3. Open GrimList and export your current dataset as a backup.
4. Open menu > Manage data > Import rules references.
5. Choose GrimList-References.json from this folder. Review and Apply update.
   Expect edition 11 and zero price changes. Keep your existing unit library.
6. Open a Death Guard test army with Virulent Vectorium selected, then Army rules.
   Check for: Putrid Detonation, Disgustingly Resilient, Plaguesurge,
   Leechspore Eruption, Overwhelming Generosity, and Creeping Blight.
7. Close/reopen GrimList and check that the cards are still there.
