Death Guard stratagem transfer test

This file restores the same edition-11 Death Guard stratagem cards already in
the Windows library. It adds no new rules text and contains no units, prices,
enhancements or detachment definitions. Other factions are not covered.

1. Before importing, use Export dataset in the phone's menu and keep the JSON.
   That export lets us establish why this phone was missing the cards. The
   phone's current database has not yet been inspected from this computer.
2. Install GrimList-Android-Test.apk over the existing app. Do not uninstall
   or clear storage: saved armies live locally on the phone.
3. Copy GrimList-DeathGuard-Stratagems.json to the phone's Downloads folder.
4. Open Manage data. Choose BSData community as the provider (to avoid the
   Official MFM provider's Points-only mode). Select edition 11 and
   Datasheets only. Leave Complete faction points OFF.
5. Choose local source and select GrimList-DeathGuard-Stratagems.json.
6. Review the preview. It must report 45 matching-edition source stratagem
   cards included for merge. Expand Detachment stratagem coverage after import:
   Death Guard / Virulent Vectorium must show 6 stratagem cards.
   Expected price changes: 0. Expected affected armies: 0.
   If an edition mismatch is reported, stop and keep your current library.
7. Tap Apply update. Open your Death Guard army with Virulent Vectorium selected.
8. Open Army rules. Confirm these six detachment cards are present:
   PUTRID DETONATION
   DISGUSTINGLY RESILIENT
   PLAGUESURGE
   LEECHSPORE ERUPTION
   OVERWHELMING GENEROSITY
   CREEPING BLIGHT
9. Confirm the missing-text message has gone, CP/text is visible, and cards from
   unselected detachments do not appear. Core stratagems remain separate.
10. Confirm units, equipment, attachments, enhancements and points are unchanged.
11. Force-stop GrimList without clearing data, reopen it, and verify the cards
    and army again. Make an ordinary edit and reopen the army to confirm saving.

An alternative is importing GrimList-Library.json with Points + datasheets,
but that applies the full supplied library. The small file above is sufficient
for restoring these references while retaining your current unit data.
